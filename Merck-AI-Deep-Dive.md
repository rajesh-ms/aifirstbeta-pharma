## Executive Summary (BLUF)

- **R&D AI is the strongest, most mature domain** — a decade-plus of AI/ML in discovery, biomarkers and trials, now being scaled enterprise-wide.
- **2026 inflection:** an **up-to-$1B** Google Cloud partnership deploys an enterprise **"agentic AI"** platform (Gemini Enterprise) across R&D, manufacturing and operations.
- **Manufacturing quality** sits on a mature digital base and is moving to predictive/gen-AI — computer-vision inspection, AI deviation management, and a 9,000-user "Visual Factory."
- **Supply-chain resilience is more visible than commonly assumed:** Merck publicly describes **gen-AI event-based risk assessments in under 30 minutes** — a genuine risk-sensing capability (correcting the frequent "blind spot" read).
- **Patents:** little **publicly visible** AI-method IP; the moat is **data + platforms + partnerships**, not patents.
- **Competitive read:** strong and on-trend, but **not clearly ahead** of Eli Lilly / Novartis on marquee discovery-AI infrastructure; Merck's edge is **enterprise-wide agentic scale + clinical-translation partnerships** (Mayo, Moderna).

## 1. R&D Productivity (core strength)

**Capabilities:** AI/ML foundation models for target identification, small-molecule and protein design, pre-lab efficacy/toxicity prediction, digital biomarkers, clinical-trial optimization, and real-world-evidence analysis.

**Quantified internal win — gen-AI document authoring (High):** an LLM platform (co-developed with McKinsey), used by **>80% of the workforce**, cut clinical-study-report first drafts from **2–3 weeks to 3–4 days** (180 → 80 hours) with **50% fewer errors**; scaling across the late-phase pipeline.

**AI in trials (High):** site selection, patient matching, and dropout-risk prediction to protect enrollment and retention.

**Flagship & specialized partnerships:**

| Partner | Focus | Terms / status | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Moderna** | V940 / mRNA-4157 (Intismeran autogene) — AI-designed *individualized* neoantigen vaccine + Keytruda | 50:50; **Phase 3** | High |
| **Mayo Clinic** | Multimodal clinical + genomic data; "virtual-cell" AI for target ID | Feb 2026; IBD, atopic dermatitis, MS | High |
| **Variational AI** | "Enki" generative small molecules vs. two undisclosed targets | Up to **$349M** | High |
| **Protillion** | "Prot-MaP" megascale data + lab-in-the-loop antibody design | Jun 2026; up to **$510M** | High |

**Why it matters:** these attack the ~1-in-10 clinical success rate and decade-long timelines — pharma's single biggest cost lever. The **Moderna** program is the marquee proof point that AI-designed, individualized medicine can reach Phase 3.

## 2. Manufacturing Quality

**Digital foundation (Low confidence on specifics):** cloud quality-management modernization and continuous-manufacturing advanced analytics (a PI System data historian feeding **Seeq**). Widely reported and consistent with Merck's posture, but not independently confirmed with a Merck-specific public citation — treat as directional.

**Current AI in quality (High):**
- **Computer vision** inspects vaccine vials/syringes for defects → less scrap, lower cost, faster lines.
- **Generative-AI deviation management** (with **AWS**) mines historical data to accelerate GMP deviation investigations.
- **"Visual Factory" on eschbach Shiftconnector** (Oct 2025): single source of truth for deviations, shift handovers and equipment tracking; **9,000+ users across global sites in <5 months**; AI surfaces institutional knowledge.

**Next step:** the Google Cloud deal explicitly targets "optimizing manufacturing through predictive analytics and intelligent automation."

**Why it matters:** quality and compliance automation protect launch cadence, cut batch loss, and shorten audit/deviation cycles — operational leverage that compounds across a large vaccine/biologic network.

## 3. Supply Chain Resilience (correcting the "blind spot")

The common read is that supply chain is Merck's weakest *digital* domain. That is **half right**:

- **It is real, and disclosed (High):** generative AI produces **event-based risk assessments in under 30 minutes** for disruptions (natural disasters, port delays), pinpointing affected products/sites so teams can act to avoid shortages. That *is* a risk-sensing capability — so the frequent "no risk-sensing system" conclusion is **inaccurate**.
- **Visual Factory** adds a real-time, unified plant view explicitly framed around "a more agile, transparent, and resilient supply chain."
- **Google Cloud** agentic ops extend resilience enterprise-wide.

**But the narrative is genuinely thin:** Merck has **no public, branded "digital supply network," control tower, or AI logistics platform** the way some peers market theirs. Capability appears present; external disclosure is limited.

**Why it matters:** faster disruption response directly protects revenue on high-value, cold-chain vaccines and biologics — and is increasingly a board-level resilience metric.

## 5. Patents / IP — *publicly visible* posture

- Public databases surface **few Merck-owned standalone AI-*method* patents**; filings are dominated by **therapeutics and formulations**.
- AI appears mainly as an **enabling tool** inside composition/biologic patents, or is held as **proprietary platforms / trade secrets** (internal gen-AI platform, foundation models). The most visible AI-linked IP is **partner-side** (e.g., Moderna's individualized-neoantigen technology behind V940).
- **Caveat:** *absence of visible AI patents ≠ absence of filings* — many may be pending/unpublished or embedded within broader claims.
- **Conclusion:** competitive advantage is driven by **execution + data + partnerships**, not an AI-patent moat.

## 7. Differentiators & Gaps

**Where Merck leads:** long-term R&D AI integration; an enterprise agentic backbone; a layered partner ecosystem; privileged clinical-translation data (Mayo); a flagship individualized-medicine program (Moderna).

**Where Merck lags or is unclear:** supply-chain AI *narrative* (capability under-disclosed); **publicly visible** AI-patent portfolio; **owned frontier compute** versus Lilly's NVIDIA "AI factory."
