Use Case 2 · Regulatory Authoring (Development) · What it is

Governed Authoring & Review Copilot

A governed last mile for regulatory writing — the place where a human reviews, signs and defends AI-drafted submissions, on the tools already on every desk (M365 Copilot · Word · Teams).

The problem
>80% of staff already draft CSRs with GPTeal + McKinsey, but pilots stall at the audit wall — the output isn’t defensible.
What it does
Assembles the evidence pack, surfaces a grounded draft in Word, and runs deterministic QA (citations, consistency, completeness) before a human ever sees it.
The principle
AI never signs. Humans never start from a blank page. Backend model interchangeable — GPTeal / Gemini / Bedrock.

Use Case 3 · Manufacturing Quality (GxP) · What it is

Gen-AI Deviation / Investigation Copilot

Inspection-ready by construction — faster GMP investigations on Azure, without migrating the multi-cloud estate. The copilot drafts; a human signs; every step is audit-clean.

The problem
