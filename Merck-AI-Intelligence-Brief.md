# Company Intelligence Brief — Merck & Co. (MSD)

### AI & Digital across R&D, Manufacturing & Supply Chain

*Public sources · as of June 2026*

> **Scope:** Merck & Co., Inc. (Rahway, NJ; "MSD" outside US/Canada). Excludes the unrelated **Merck KGaA** (AIDDISON, BenevolentAI/Exscientia, Palantir-Athinia).

**Three most material moves**

- **Google Cloud** (Apr 2026) — *up to* **$1B**, multi-year; **Gemini Enterprise** agentic platform across R&D, manufacturing, commercial and corporate functions.
- **Moderna — V940 / mRNA-4157** (Intismeran autogene) — AI-designed *individualized* cancer vaccine + Keytruda; now in **Phase 3**.
- **Mayo Clinic** (Feb 2026) — multimodal clinical + genomic data and "virtual-cell" AI for target identification.

---

## 1. R&D Productivity

- **Foundation models** for target discovery, molecular design, and pre-lab efficacy/toxicity prediction.
- **Internal gen-AI platform** (>80% of staff): clinical study report first drafts cut **2–3 weeks → 3–4 days**, **50% fewer errors**.
- **AI in trials:** site selection, patient matching, dropout-risk prediction.
- Discovery partners: **Variational AI** (generative small molecules), **Protillion** (antibodies).
- **Why it matters:** directly targets the \~1-in-10 clinical success rate and decade-long timelines — pharma's single biggest cost lever.

## 2. Manufacturing Quality

- **Computer vision** defect inspection of vials/syringes (less scrap, faster lines).
- **Generative-AI deviation management** (with **AWS**) → faster GMP investigations.
- **Visual Factory / eschbach Shiftconnector** (Oct 2025): **9,000+ users** in under 5 months; single source of truth for deviations and handovers.
- **Why it matters:** quality automation protects launch cadence and cuts batch loss and compliance exposure.

## 3. Supply Chain Resilience

- **Gen-AI risk assessments in <30 minutes** flag products/sites hit by disruptions (disasters, port delays).
- Real-time **Visual Factory** plant view; **Google Cloud** agentic ops extend resilience enterprise-wide.
- **Why it matters:** faster disruption response protects revenue on high-value vaccines and biologics.

## 4. Patents / IP — *publicly visible* posture

Few **publicly visible** Merck-owned AI-*method* patents; AI is largely held as **proprietary platforms / trade secrets**, while most AI-linked patents sit **partner-side** (e.g., Moderna's neoantigen technology). **Moat = data + platforms + partnerships, not AI patents.**

---

## Strategic Implications

- Merck is shifting from point tools to an **enterprise "agentic" backbone** — a potential durable speed/cost edge **if** the Google Cloud rollout delivers at scale.
- A **partnership-led** model (cloud + clinical + biotech) buys capability fast but creates **dependency** on third parties.
- Versus Pfizer, Novartis and Lilly, Merck looks **on-trend, not clearly ahead**; differentiation will come from **execution and proprietary data**, not IP.

## Key Risks

- **Regulatory acceptance** of AI-generated documents/decisions (FDA/EMA).
- **Data privacy & governance** (Mayo clinical/genomic and patient data).
- **Model governance in GMP** (validated state, audit trails, model drift).
- **Partner / vendor dependency** (Google Cloud lock-in; Moderna program risk).

## Watchlist (6–18 months)

1. Google Cloud / Gemini rollout progress and **realized** productivity gains.
2. **Phase 3 V940 / mRNA-4157** efficacy readouts and regulatory path.
3. **Measurable** manufacturing gains (yield, deviation cycle time, scrap rate).
