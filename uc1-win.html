The real risk

The risk isn’t committing to the program. It’s pilot purgatory.

Standalone POCs that prove a point but never scale — 12–18 months lost to re-mobilization, momentum, and budget cycles, while the cliff gets closer. The answer is speed with commitment, made safe by gates.

Commit to the program vs pilot-first

Standalone pilot first
  • Re-mobilize team + procurement after every step
  • Momentum and context lost between funding cycles
  • Value deferred 12–18 mo; cliff keeps approaching
  • Foundation rebuilt, not reused
Committed program, gated
  • One mobilization; team continuity across phases
  • Pilot delivered inside P1 — with a G1 stop
  • Fastest path to portfolio-scale value
  • Every phase has a customer off-ramp (G0–G4)
Same downside protection as a pilot — without the stalls. You can stop at any gate; you only lose the stalls, not the momentum.
