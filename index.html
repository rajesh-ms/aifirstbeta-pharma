Answering the readiness standard: “What do you know about this customer’s business pressures — not their technology, their business?”

Team Pharma  ·  June 24, 2026

Customer Pressure Brief

ElementNotes
One-line problem statement Merck must get medicines to market faster without losing audit credibility with the FDA/EMA — just as the 2028 Keytruda patent cliff (~$29.5B, ~46% of 2024 sales) forces it to defend revenue, cost-of-goods and speed-to-patient at once. (BioSpace, Sep 2025; Pharmaceutical Technology / JPM26, Jan 2026)
2–3 measurable business outcomes
  • Clinical study report (CSR) cycle: 2–3 weeks → 3–4 days; human-reviewed first draft 180→80 hrs, 50% fewer errors — Merck + McKinsey QuantumBlack (McKinsey/Merck, Jun 2025).
  • Trial cost & manual effort cut ~70% with ~90% accuracy across 300+ concurrent trials — AWS Clinical Trial Optimization Studio (AWS case study, 2025).
  • Batch-record documentation errors down ~70% (Acodis, Dec 2025).
Top 5 business pressures
  1. AI-at-scale arms race — P. Hudson, CEO Sanofi: “…become the first pharma company powered by AI at scale” (Sanofi, Jun 2023).
  2. Submission speed = revenue — T. Coleman, CIO Med. Dev., Lilly: “Each day a drug gets to market sooner creates $1–3M in accelerated revenue” (Yseop, 2026).
  3. Scientists stuck copyediting — R. Kim, CTO Merck: “Some of our scientists were taking time being copyeditors. That’s not what they trained for” (NAM, Mar 2025).
  4. Investigations & documentation are top regulatory pain — FDA 2024 data: both remain top-five most-cited deficiencies (ISPE iSpeak, Nov 2025).
  5. Data integrity gates everything — “Efficiency must go hand in hand with validation, traceability and human accountability” (Rephine, Sep 2025).
Strategy vs execution tensions (1–2)
  • Paper-compliant pilots ≠ process-healthy reality: “a fast answer is worth nothing if it cannot be trusted and traced… a confident summary it cannot source… has handed [QA] a new liability” (Fierce Pharma, Jun 2026).
  • Value capture is rare: only 24% of companies have created value from GenAI — just 10% in medtech (BCG, Mar 2025).
What has already been tried?
  • Merck CSR automation w/ McKinsey QuantumBlack (180→80 hrs, 50% fewer errors), scaling across late-stage pipeline (eMarketer, Aug 2025).
  • Deviation management GenAI on AWS Bedrock + OpenSearch (RAG) over past deviations, “maintaining the rigorous standards demanded by GMP” (AWS/MSD, Nov 2025).
  • GPTeal secure enterprise LLM platform (NAM, Mar 2025); batch records ~70% fewer doc errors (Acodis, Dec 2025).
  • Competitors: AstraZeneca protocol tool across 240+ trials; Bayer coding LLM 96% accuracy under “four-eyes”; Pfizer “Charlie.”

Provocations & opening questions

Bold provocations
  1. Every week you cut from a filing is revenue — until a regulator asks how. Can you explain the “how” as fast as you moved?
  2. Where are you winning the audit but losing the weeks — “compliant” but still slow?
  3. Rivals file in days, not weeks. Are you re-engineering the work — or just paying more to be fast at a broken process?
Top 3 questions to ask first
  1. What’s your release-to-filing cycle time today — and what would halving it be worth in revenue and working capital?
  2. Where does slow, manual work cost you most — release, investigations, or documentation?
  3. What has to be true on trust and auditability before you’d let those speed gains scale?
