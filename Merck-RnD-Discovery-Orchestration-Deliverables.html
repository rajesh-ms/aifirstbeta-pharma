Deliverable 2  ·  Persona-Driven Scenarios

Human + AI Collaboration — Discovery Personas

Decisions the copilot supports — not replaces — across the discovery pipeline.

Framing template

“An AI copilot that assists <persona> by synthesizing <data>, flagging <risks>, and recommending <actions> — while the human retains accountability.”

DS
Discovery scientist / computational chemist
R&D · small-molecule + target

Synthesizes target + Mayo multimodal data, flags non-synthesizable or toxic candidates pre-lab, and recommends a ranked shortlist — human retains scientific accountability.

Agent does: Runs target → design → dock → triage; returns ranked candidates with evidence & lineage.
Supports (not replaces): Which candidates advance to wet-lab; target go / no-go.
Human + AI: AI triages at portfolio scale; scientist validates biology and approves the shortlist (four-eyes).
New roles / interfaces: In-silico triage scientist · agent workbench over Foundry · HPC cost steward.
AE
Antibody / protein-design engineer
R&D · biologics (Protillion)

Synthesizes sequence + structure + developability data, flags immunogenicity & liability risks, and recommends optimized binders — human retains design accountability.

Agent does: Orchestrates antibody LMs + generative design + ABodyBuilder structure + developability/immunogenicity screens.
Supports (not replaces): Which scaffolds / sequences to synthesize; potency-vs-developability trade-offs.
Human + AI: AI proposes & optimizes; engineer judges manufacturability and approves designs.
New roles / interfaces: Model-catalog curator · biologics agent-ops engineer.
